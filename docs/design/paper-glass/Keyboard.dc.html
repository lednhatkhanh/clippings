Focus you can always see

A 2px ring in the accent’s text colour, 2px out, following each shape’s radius. It shows on [data-focus-visible] only, so mouse clicks stay quiet, and it never relies on colour alone.

Hints, not clutter

The screen stays calm: only ⌘K in the rail and a quiet ↵ on the focused row. Every other shortcut lives in tooltips, the ⌘K palette and this sheet, and none show on touch screens.
