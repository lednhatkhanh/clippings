Motion · pages

The glass stays, the paper changes

On every navigation the frosted frame (rail, header, tab bar) holds perfectly still. Only the paper underneath moves: it fades and drifts a few pixels in the direction you went. Going deeper slides forward; going back reverses it. Typing, filtering and sorting never animate a page; results swap in place.

Between sections

Click the rail. Lower in the rail drifts up, higher drifts down.
